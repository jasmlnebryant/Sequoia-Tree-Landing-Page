Stairs on the short path around the General Grant Tree; they can be bypassed to finish the loop
Distance
0.3 mi (0.5 km) loop
Duration
About 20–30 min (estimate)
Slope or elevation information
Steep in places; not designated wheelchair-accessible
Accessible parking
One accessible space at the trailhead
Nearby facilities
Accessible flush restrooms and drinking fountains
