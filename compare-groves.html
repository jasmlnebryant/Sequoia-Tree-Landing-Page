Walking options

Main Sherman Tree lot off Wolverton Road; free summer shuttle from Giant Forest Museum
Accessibility details
Paved accessible trail to General Sherman (about 500 ft); Big Trees Trail is paved with boardwalk

Grant Grove

Discover General Grant and the surrounding grove.

Park
Kings Canyon National Park
Featured landmark
General Grant Tree
Walking options
General Grant Tree Trail (0.3 mi paved loop); Panoramic Point (0.5 mi, seasonal)
Arrival and parking information
General Grant Tree lot near Grant Grove Village, via Hwy 180 and the Big Stump entrance
Accessibility details
Paved, with some steeper stretches. For a step-free route, see the Big Trees Trail. Tactile and Braille panels along the trail.
